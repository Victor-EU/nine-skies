## What it cannot decide

**3,000 basins, 143,342 km²,** are touched by no mapped river or lake. At
1:10 million that is most of them, and it is the half of the question F59
said Natural Earth could not answer. A lake with no mapped river is not a
sink either: the network is too coarse to draw every outflow, so a lake
that drains through a river this map leaves out reads exactly like one
that does not drain at all. D62 settles both the way HydroSHEDS settled
the sinks it did not inspect: the untouched basins are filled, and the
lakes this map draws are kept at their level.
